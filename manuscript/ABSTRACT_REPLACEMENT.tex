\begin{abstract}
We introduce \emph{Path-Complete Closure Certificates} (PC-CCs) for verifying persistence of discrete-time nonlinear switched systems under arbitrary switching. A PC-CC is a graph-indexed family of relational functions over state pairs, coupled by local one-step and transitive-closure implications and a well-founded descent condition on a finite-visit set. We prove that path-completeness is necessary and sufficient for the soundness of these local conditions over all switched systems: every PC-CC on a path-complete graph certifies persistence, whereas a non-path-complete graph admits a spurious certificate for some nonpersistent system. Persistence is an infinite-horizon property of the form $\mathbf F\mathbf G\,\neg\mathcal X_{\mathrm{vf}}$, so the relational construction permits finitely many transient visits while excluding recurrent behavior. On the nonlinear two-car platoon and the submitted two-node graph, an explicit degree-two/four graph-indexed closure family together with an affine recurrent rank certifies a four-visit persistence instance through strict SOS feasibility. A separate dimension-scalable graph-indexed benchmark directly exercises the original PC-CC3 condition non-vacuously over four visits using degree-two relational certificates, with exact positive margin $475/3904$. All reported numerical SOS blocks satisfy strict \texttt{OPTIMAL}/\texttt{FEASIBLE\_POINT} acceptance criteria, and the accompanying repository provides deterministic reproduction scripts.
\end{abstract}
